Distinations

Swat

Swat Valley, the “Switzerland of Pakistan,”
charms visitors with its lush green valleys,
crystal-clear rivers, ancient Buddhist
heritage sites, and snow-capped peaks
perfect for trekking and nature escapes.


Hunza

Hunza Valley captivates with its majestic peaks,
historic forts, apricot orchards, crystal-clear
Attabad Lake, and warm hospitality amid some of
the world's most stunning mountain scenery.


Muree

Murree, a classic hill station near Islamabad,
delights with pine-covered hills, cool breezes,
colonial-era charm, and easy access to scenic
spots like Patriata and Mall Road.


Skardu

Skardu, the gateway to the mighty Karakoram,
offers breathtaking views of towering mountains,
serene lakes like Satpara and Shangrila, and
adventures toward K2 and Deosai Plains.


Naran

Naran, nestled in the Kaghan Valley,
is a paradise of alpine meadows, the turquoise
Saiful Muluk Lake, and refreshing mountain air
ideal for summer retreats and nature walks.


Kaghan

Kaghan Valley enchants with its dramatic landscapes,
rushing rivers, lush forests, and iconic spots like
Lulusar Lake and Babusar Pass, making it a top
destination for scenic drives and outdoor adventure.
